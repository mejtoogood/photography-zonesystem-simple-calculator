How to use

  1. Spot-meter a subject with your camera. Select the metered shutter speed, aperture and ISO above the tapes.
  2. Tap the zone where you want that subject to appear. The highlighted row is your metered tone; swipe the EV or Exposure tape to move its placement.
  3. Set your camera to the shutter/aperture combination on Zone V (bold outline), at your selected ISO. The other rows are equivalent readings for subjects placed in those zones.
  4. To check another part of the scene, spot-meter it at the same ISO and aperture. Find that reading on the Exposure tape to see which zone it would occupy.

Metering a shadow

Meter the darkest shadow needing clear texture. For example, at 1/30 · f/4 · ISO 400, place it in Zone III. Shoot at roughly 1/125 · f/4 · ISO 400 (Zone V row).

Metering a highlight

Meter a bright area you want to retain texture in. For example, at 1/1000 · f/4 · ISO 400, place it in Zone VIII for a very bright highlight with minimal texture. Shoot at roughly 1/125 · f/4 · ISO 400 (Zone V row). For more highlight texture, consider Zone VII.

Important

The camera meter treats the measured area as a midtone (Zone V) before placement. These are exposure relationships, not guarantees of highlight detail or shadow texture: film, development and digital sensor response matter.
